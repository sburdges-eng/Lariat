August 2026 checklists

The Lariat · Daily · Weekly · Monthly cleaning & side work

Daily = every close · Weekly = Wed→Sun · Monthly target = Sun Aug 16 (makeup Sun Aug 23)

1. Daily (every close)

Line

  • Restock / top-fill station
  • Flip station
  • Refill sauce + squeeze bottles
  • Wrap line
  • Wipe cutting boards
  • Sanitize prep sink, knives, surfaces
  • Clean stovetop / char / flat top (grill)
  • Season flat top (grill)
  • Filter fryer oil (fry)
  • Ice-bath + wrap steam well (expo)
  • Empty grease trays

House

  • Equipment off (steam, grill, fryer, oven)
  • Restock reach-in
  • Wipe stainless
  • Dishes washed + machine drained
  • Sweep + mop; empty mop bucket
  • Gas to wok off
  • Hood vent off
  • Trash out · rag buckets emptied
