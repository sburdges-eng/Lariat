Prep cascade

WED 9/2 — today

  • Pull beef cheeks from the freezer to thaw. Three-day lead. If this slips, the barbacoa does not happen.

THU 9/3

  • Barbacoa / birria — 32 qt (two 16 qt batches). Season cheeks with QB seasoning, sear all sides, split into two deep hotel pans, 225°F for 8–12 hours fatty side up. Cool and store. Strain the consommé through a chinois.
  • Pull chicken legs to thaw
  • QB seasoning — 3 cup
  • Lariat rub — 3 cup
  • Aji verde — 3.2 qt
  • Chipotle aioli — 5 qt

FRI 9/4

  • Chicken confit — 6 hotel pans. Green salt both sides, submerge in oil with garlic, rosemary, thyme. Foil, 225°F for 3 hours. Refrigerate right away.
  • Queso / mac sauce — 11 qt (ice wand to cool)
  • Blackened tomato salsa — 11 qt
  • Green chile — 8 qt
  • Mexi slaw — 10 lb
  • Beer flour dry mix — 9 cup
  • Cut and portion the fish
  • Elote — wash and cut
  • Churro dough

SAT 9/5 — day of

  • Pico de gallo — 8 qt, made fresh today
  • Onion-cilantro garnish
  • Fish brine — 4 qt. Brine 7 minutes only, then drain and cut.
  • Elote — toss, dress, build 4 pans
  • Reheat barbacoa and chicken to 165°F. Log it.
  • Beer batter — wet into dry at service, not before
