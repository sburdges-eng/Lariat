Sysco order guide — count sheet

Count date: __________ Counted by: __________ Order placed: ☐ Sun ☐ Wed

Walk the building in this order. Count Have in cases. Order = Par − Have (never below zero); add event needs on top.

Uses/wk = real service usage per week from your Sysco invoices, with banquet buying stripped out: orders placed in the 5 days before a booked event (Rodeo 6/3, Howell wedding 7/17, Drewek 7/22) are capped at that item's normal order size before the rate is computed. Items new to Sysco are rated over their own active span. Event needs go on top of par from the BEO — never into it.

Par = one full week of that usage. Since you count twice a week (Sun + Wed), par carries roughly double the cover you need between drops — that margin is the safety stock. Pencil it as you learn.

Items you have not ordered in 6 months are listed at the end, off the count.
